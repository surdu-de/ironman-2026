## Where the 6h Bike Goal Lives in Reality

Wed Apr 22 outdoor (76km / 27.7 km/h / HR 142) is the most honest data point.

To average 30 km/h sustained for 180km, all of these need to land:

| Lever | Status | Realistic gain |
|---|---|---|
| TT bike + practiced aero | Have bike, need position refinement | ~2-3 min/h |
| FTP +15-25W (240 → 255-265) | 6 weeks possible | +10-15W likely, +25W rare |
| -4kg body weight (89 → 85) | Discipline required | 30-60 sec total |
| Race-pace HR 138-142 sustained | **Untested at 5h+** | The hardest piece |
| Course tactics (legal draft, even pace) | Practice in Wk26-27 | 5-10 min |

**Compound math**: all levers at upper end → 6:15-6:25. **6:00 requires every lever to land at the upper end of plausible.**

A more honest target: **6:20 bike split** (27.5 km/h at HR 138). Lands around 13:00-13:10 total. Slightly slower than 12:50 stretch but far higher probability.

## The Single Biggest Risk

**Bike-run pacing tradeoff on race day.** Most age-grouper PR attempts die here.

- **6:25 bike (HR 138 avg)** → solid run start, marathon executes
- **6:00 bike (HR 145 avg)** → run starts on a debt that compounds, blow-up risk

The split that protects the day: **train for 6:00 fitness, race at 6:25 wisdom**. Total time often the same, lower blow-up risk. Don't fight this on race day.

## Risks of the 4-Build-No-Recovery Plan

Chose the more aggressive structure. Real risks:

4. **Wk29 taper has to drop hard**: 100% → 56% → 44% works on paper. If arriving at Wk29 cooked, no taper saves you.
